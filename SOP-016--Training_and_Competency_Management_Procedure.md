## 1.0 PURPOSE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes how __NewInstitute__
determines that personnel are competent for the roles they hold, and how it
evidences that personnel know the procedures under which they work.

This procedure implements the training and competency requirements of
[QM-001 Section 6.1.1](QM-001--QualityManual.md#611-people) and addresses the
competence and awareness requirements of ISO-9001:2015 Sections 7.2 and 7.3.

This procedure ensures that:

- Competence is determined from documented credentials before an individual
  performs activities governed by the QMS
- Each individual attests to having read and understood the procedures
  assigned to their role
- Attestations are refreshed when an assigned document is substantively
  revised

## 2.0 SCOPE

This procedure applies to all __NewInstitute__ personnel who perform
activities governed by this QMS, including every individual holding a role
defined in QM-001 Appendix A.

Training under this procedure consists of assigned reading, a signed
attestation, and an offered discussion with the individual's direct
reporting superior. Examinations and graded competency assessments are not
part of this model; the basis of competence is the credential record
described in Section 4.3.

This procedure does **NOT** apply to:

- Personnel of external organizations, whose qualification is addressed in
  [SOP-006: Outsourced Activities Procedure](SOP-006--Outsourced_Activities_Procedure.md)
  Section 4.2
- The named qualification requirements that apply to the
  Sponsor-Investigator, which are addressed in
  [SOP-001: Design Controls Procedure](SOP-001--Design_Controls_Procedure.md)
  Section 4.2.2
- Auditor competence criteria, which are addressed in
  [SOP-012: Internal and External Audit Procedure](SOP-012--Internal_and_External_Audit_Procedure.md)
  Section 7.1
- Study-specific and site training delivered as part of design transfer,
  which is addressed in SOP-001 Section 5.8.4

The requirements in those sections are additional to, and are not replaced
by, this procedure.

Records created under this procedure are controlled under
[SOP-002: Document and Records Controls Procedure](SOP-002--Document_and_Records_Controls_Procedure.md).

### 3.3 Terms

- **Attestation**: A signed statement by an individual that they have read a
  specified revision of an assigned document, understand its requirements as
  they apply to their role, and had the opportunity to discuss the document
  with their direct reporting superior.

- **Qualification Record**: The set of credentials held on file for an
  individual that establishes their competence for the role they hold,
  comprising a current curriculum vitae together with the degrees, licenses,
  and certifications relevant to that role.

- **Training Assignment Matrix**: A controlled document mapping each role
  defined in QM-001 Appendix A to the QMS documents that a person holding
  that role is required to read.

- **Training Record**: The record of attestations completed by an individual,
  identifying each assigned document, the revision read, and the dates of
  attestation and countersignature.

## 4.0 PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Overview

Competence and training are evidenced by two records maintained for each
individual:

- A Qualification Record, holding the credentials that establish the
  individual is competent for the role they hold (Section 4.3)
- A Training Record, holding the attestations that evidence the individual
  has read and understood the procedures assigned to that role
  (Sections 4.4 and 4.5)

__NewInstitute__ determines competence from documented credentials rather
than from examination. Written tests, graded competency assessments, and
proficiency scoring are not required by this procedure. Where a specific
role requires a named course or credential, that requirement is stated in
the procedure that owns the role, as listed in Section 2.0.

This model is proportionate to a single-site institute conducting
*observational studies* with a small team, in which the direct reporting
superior of each individual is positioned to confirm understanding through
discussion rather than through testing.

### 4.2 Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| Executive Management | Ensures personnel meet minimum levels of competence per QM-001 §6.1.1 and provides the resources required for training. Countersigns attestations where an individual has no direct reporting superior within __NewInstitute__ |
| Management Representative | Maintains the Training Assignment Matrix and the institute training file. Confirms that a Qualification Record and the required attestations exist before an individual is assigned to a role |
| QA Representative | Samples Qualification Records and Training Records during internal audits per SOP-012 |
| Direct reporting superior | Makes themselves available to discuss assigned documents with the individual, and countersigns each attestation |
| Individual | Reads assigned documents, signs attestations, and keeps their CV and credentials current |

### 4.3 Qualification Record

Before an individual performs activities governed by this QMS, a
Qualification Record **SHALL** be established and held in the institute
training file. The Qualification Record **SHALL** contain:

- A current CV;
- The degrees, licenses, and professional certifications relevant to the
  role the individual holds; and
- Any additional credential required for that role by another procedure, as
  listed in Section 2.0.

The Qualification Record is the documented basis for the determination that
the individual is competent for the role. This procedure creates no course
or certification requirement beyond those already stated in the procedures
that own each role.

The individual **SHALL** provide an updated CV or credential when a change
relevant to the role they hold occurs.

### 4.4 Training Assignment

The Management Representative **SHALL** maintain a Training Assignment
Matrix. The Training Assignment Matrix is a controlled document and
**SHALL** be created, approved, and revised per SOP-002.

The Training Assignment Matrix **SHALL**:

- Assign QM-001 to every individual performing activities governed by this
  QMS;
- Assign each SOP to the population identified in the TRAINING section of
  that SOP; and
- Identify, for each role, any work instruction or study-level plan that the
  role is required to read.

Assignments **SHALL** be reviewed when an individual is assigned to a role,
when an individual's role changes, and when a new controlled document is
released.

### 4.5 Attestation

Before performing an activity governed by an assigned document, the
individual **SHALL** sign an attestation recording that they have:

- Read the assigned document at its current revision;
- Understood its requirements as they apply to their role; and
- Had the opportunity to discuss the document with their direct reporting
  superior.

The direct reporting superior **SHALL** countersign the attestation,
confirming that the opportunity to discuss was offered. Where an individual
has no direct reporting superior within __NewInstitute__, Executive
Management **SHALL** countersign.

Each attestation **SHALL** record:

- The name and role of the individual;
- The identifier and revision of the document attested to;
- The date the document was read;
- The signature and date of the individual; and
- The signature and date of the countersigner.

Attestations are recorded in the individual's Training Record. Signatures
**SHALL** meet the requirements of SOP-002 Section 5.3.2.

### 4.6 Re-attestation on Revision

When a controlled document is revised and released per SOP-002 Section 4.5,
the approver **SHALL** determine whether the revision is substantive for the
purposes of this section, and **SHALL** record that determination in the
Record of Revisions of the revised document.

A revision is substantive when it changes what personnel do, who performs an
activity, or what is recorded. A revision that corrects typography,
formatting, or cross-references without changing a requirement is editorial.

Personnel to whom a substantively revised document is assigned **SHALL**
complete a fresh attestation before they next perform an activity governed
by that document. An editorial revision does not require re-attestation.

An individual assigned to a new role **SHALL** attest to the documents that
role carries and to which they have not previously attested at the current
revision.

### 4.7 Record Ownership and Location

Qualification Records and Training Records are quality records. They
**SHALL** be held in the institute training file maintained by the
Management Representative, and controlled per SOP-002.

Where a study requires evidence of the qualifications or training of study
personnel, a copy **SHALL** be filed in that study's TMF per SOP-001
Section 7.2.

Records created under this procedure **SHALL** be retained per SOP-002
Section 5.4.

## 5.0 TRAINING

Personnel who perform activities governed by this procedure **SHALL** be
trained on it before performing those activities, through the assignment and
attestation process of Sections 4.4 and 4.5.

Training records are maintained per Section 6.0.

## APPENDIX A: RECORD TEMPLATES

The templates in this appendix render the requirements of Sections 4.3, 4.4,
and 4.5 into usable forms. The body sections cited are canonical; where a
template and the body differ, the body governs. Templates **MAY** be adapted
to __NewInstitute__'s circumstances, provided the required content is
preserved.

### A.1 Qualification Record Template

One Qualification Record per individual:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Name | Full name of the individual |
| Role(s) held | Roles per QM-001 Appendix A, with effective dates |
| CV | Reference to the current CV on file, and its date |
| Degrees | Degree, awarding institution, date awarded |
| Licenses and certifications | Type, issuing body, identifier, expiry date where applicable |
| Additional role credentials | Credentials required by another procedure per §2.0, with a reference to their verification |
| Competence determination | Statement that the credentials above establish competence for the role, with the name and date of the person making the determination |

### A.2 Training Record Template

One Training Record per individual, with one row per attestation:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Document ID | Identifier of the assigned document |
| Revision | Revision attested to |
| Date read | Date the individual completed reading the document |
| Individual signature / date | Attestation per §4.5 |
| Countersignature / date | Direct reporting superior, confirming the opportunity to discuss was offered |
| Trigger | Initial assignment, role change, or substantive revision per §4.6 |
